# Steevane Crosspost – Privacy Policy

Steevane Crosspost is a personal automation tool operated by Steven Akinola (Steevane) for his own social media accounts. Last updated: 13 November 2026.

## What the app does

When the owner publishes a new Reel on his own Instagram account, the app copies that video to the owner's own YouTube channel and sends it to the owner's own TikTok inbox as a draft.

## Information the app accesses

The owner's own Instagram videos and captions, to copy them to his other accounts. Authorisation tokens for the owner's own Instagram, YouTube and TikTok accounts.

## How information is used and stored

Videos are downloaded temporarily to be uploaded and are then deleted. Tokens and a list of already-posted videos are stored in a private code repository controlled by the owner. The app does not collect information about any other person, does not sell or share data, and does not show ads.

## Third parties

The app communicates only with Instagram (Meta), YouTube (Google) and TikTok, using their official APIs. Their own privacy policies apply to data on their platforms.

## Your choices

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from the security settings of each platform, which immediately stops the app.
